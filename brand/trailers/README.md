# Reckon trailers

Two channel trailers in the Margin note style: Lora wordmark, a yellow pencil loop (#F4C430) round the key number,
graphite #2E2E33 on paper #FAFAF7, Patrick Hand notes. Both are 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC, about −16 LUFS.

| File | Length | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `reckon-trailer.mp4` | 33 s | The calm one. Frames from the videos land on the page one by one, each with its loop and question, then the camera pulls back to a heap of frames: "Big questions, worked out." Ends on the logo sting, "Reckon. Look closer." |
| `reckon-trailer-fast.mp4` | 27 s | The fast cut, timed to the beat. Questions scrawled on the page, frames punching in on their key numbers, "Big questions. Worked out.", the pull-back to the heap, then the music stops for "Reckon… look closer." |

The frames are examples from the long-form videos; the heap behind them is made from other moments of the same videos.
The scenes and the sound mixes are in `longform/brand-trailer/` and `longform/brand-trailer2/`, with the heap in
`longform/brand/pile.js`.
